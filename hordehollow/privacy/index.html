Horde Hollow · Privacy Policy

What Horde Hollow keeps about you, and how to delete it

Horde Hollow is a zombie base-building game for Android made by Printed Theories. This page explains the small amount of data the game stores so your camp, friends and progress work online.

Effective October 30, 2026Developer: Printed Theories

What we collect

You don't sign up with an email or password. The game creates an anonymous account the first time you play. We store:

DataExampleWhy
Player IDA random ID made by the gameIdentifies your account so your save loads on your devices
Player nameThe nickname you choose, like Dekoda#4821Shown to other players in rankings, squads, attacks and friend search
Game progressYour camp layout, buildings, resources, heroes, trophies, missionsCloud save, and so other players can attack or visit your camp
Friends listFriends, friend requests, blocked players, squad invitesThe Friends feature
Last seen timeWhen you last had the game openShows friends whether you're online

We don't collect your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or payment details. The game has no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.

How it's used

  • To save your progress and restore it on another device.
  • To run online features: attacks on other camps, the trophy rankings, co-op squads, and friends.
  • To stop cheating and abuse, for example by checking attack results on the server and letting you block players.

We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising.

Who sees it

  • Other players can see your player name, trophies, camp level and camp layout, whether you're online (if you're friends), and your camp when they attack or visit it.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) hosts the game's accounts, cloud saves, rankings and multiplayer on our behalf. Unity processes this data only to provide those services. See Unity's privacy policy.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We keep it until you delete your account.

Age

Horde Hollow is meant for players 13 and older. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Delete your Horde Hollow account

You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the game:

  1. Open Horde Hollow and tap SETTINGS.
  2. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. Tap DELETE FOREVER to confirm. You need to be online.

This permanently deletes your player ID, name, camp, progress, trophies and ranking, friends list and cloud save. You're removed from other players' friend lists and from name search. It can't be undone. The game then starts over on a new account.

If you can't open the game anymore, email us from any address with your player name (the full name with its # number, shown in Settings) and ask for deletion. We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

Emailprintedtheories.official@gmail.com

Nothing is kept after deletion, except that copies may stay in the hosting provider's backups for a short time before they're overwritten.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that collect more data (for example in-app purchases or clan chat), we'll update this page and the effective date above before they ship.
